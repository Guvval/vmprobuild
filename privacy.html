Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

VM PROBUILD ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how we protect it. It is written in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us when you fill in an enquiry form on this website:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Location of your plot

If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, we will also have the details you share in that conversation, such as your email address, project requirements, budget or plot documents.

We do not ask for payment details, Aadhaar, PAN or bank information through this website.

2. How we use your information

  • To call or message you back about your construction enquiry, usually within 20 minutes during working hours.
  • To arrange a free consultation and site visit, and to prepare an estimate or quotation.
  • To share project updates, documents and payment information if you become our customer.
  • To keep records required for accounting, tax and legal purposes.

We will not use your details for unrelated marketing, and we will never sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

3. Your consent

By submitting a form on this website, you consent to VM PROBUILD contacting you by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email about your enquiry. You can withdraw this consent at any time by telling us (see "Contact us" below). After that, we will stop contacting you, except where we must keep records by law.

4. Who we share it with

Your information is seen only by the VM PROBUILD team handling your enquiry. To run this website we use a small number of trusted service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers the enquiry form to our email inbox.
  • Netlify, which hosts this website.
  • Google (Gmail), where enquiry emails are received.

Some of these providers may store data on servers outside India. We may also disclose information if required by law, a court order or a government authority.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking tools. Our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running.

6. How long we keep it

If you do not go ahead with a project, we delete your enquiry details within 12 months. For customers, we keep project and billing records for as long as required by tax and other applicable laws.

7. Keeping your data safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including secure (HTTPS) form submission and limiting access to our team. However, no method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, where we are not required to keep it by law;
  • stop contacting you.

We will respond to your request within 30 days.

9. Children

This website is meant for adults planning construction projects. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
